Welcome to my blog. This space is for sharing my thoughts, tutorials, and experiences as a software engineer.

## What to expect

I'll be writing about:

- **Web development** — Vue.js, Nuxt, and Node.js.
- **Mobile development** — the tools and patterns behind building applications.
- **Security** — thinking about how to keep applications safe.

Work is one part of the picture. There's room here for life away from the keyboard, too.

## A small example

This website is built with Nuxt and Vue. A component can start with something as simple as a title and a little markup:

```vue
<script setup>
const title = "A place to share what I learn";
</script>

<template>
  <section>
    <h2>{{ title }}</h2>
    <p>One note at a time.</p>
  </section>
</template>
```

The title is ordinary text, rendered directly into the page. It's a small starting point for a space that can grow over time.
